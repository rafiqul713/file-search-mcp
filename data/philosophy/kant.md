# Immanuel Kant: The Transcendental Turn

Kant's "Copernican Revolution" in philosophy argued that the mind is not a passive receiver of sensory data, but an active participant that structures our experience.

## Core Concepts
1. **The Categorical Imperative:** A moral law that is unconditional or absolute for all agents. "Act only according to that maxim whereby you can at the same time will that it should become a universal law."
2. **Phenomena vs. Noumena:** The distinction between things as they appear to us (phenomena) versus things as they are in themselves (noumena/thing-in-itself).
3. **Synthetic A Priori:** Knowledge that is informative (synthetic) yet necessary/universal (a priori), such as mathematical truths.
