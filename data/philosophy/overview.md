# German Philosophy Overview

German philosophy has historically dominated Western thought, particularly from the late 18th century through the 20th century. It is characterized by its systematic nature, focus on the subject-object relationship, and its deep influence on social theory, psychology, and modern politics.

## Key Eras
* **German Idealism:** Late 18th to mid-19th century (Kant, Fichte, Schelling, Hegel).
* **Existentialism & Phenomenology:** Late 19th to 20th century (Nietzsche, Husserl, Heidegger).
* **Critical Theory:** The Frankfurt School (Adorno, Horkheimer, Habermas).
