# Existence and Critical Theory

This section covers the shift from questioning "knowledge" to questioning "existence" and "society."

## Martin Heidegger
Heidegger, in *Being and Time*, focused on "Dasein" (Being-there). He argued that we are "thrown" into a world we did not choose. He was deeply critical of modern technology, arguing that it "enframes" nature, treating everything—including humans—as mere standing reserves of resources for industrial use.

## The Frankfurt School (Critical Theory)
Thinkers like Adorno, Horkheimer, and Habermas analyzed how culture and society shape human behavior.
* **The Culture Industry:** They argued that mass-produced culture serves to pacify the population, preventing them from critical engagement with the structures of power.
* **Rationalization:** The process where social life becomes increasingly focused on efficiency and technical control, often at the expense of human meaning.
