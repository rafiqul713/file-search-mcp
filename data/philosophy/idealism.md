# German Idealism: From Kant to Hegel

German Idealism represents the bridge between the Enlightenment and Modernity, focusing on the active role of the mind in shaping reality.

## Immanuel Kant
Kant argued that space, time, and causality are not things that exist "out there" in the world, but are the "lens" through which the human mind perceives everything. His famous Categorical Imperative dictates that one should only act according to maxims that could be willed as a universal law.

## G.W.F. Hegel
Hegel moved beyond Kant by suggesting that truth is not static. He introduced the "Dialectic," a process where an idea (Thesis) meets its opposite (Antithesis), resulting in a higher, more complex truth (Synthesis). He viewed history as the development of Spirit (Geist) toward the realization of freedom.
