# Friedrich Nietzsche: The Challenge to Tradition

Nietzsche is the primary figure of the "Death of God" movement, arguing that traditional religious and moral foundations have collapsed.

## Core Concepts
* **The Will to Power:** The fundamental driving force in all humans—the desire to grow, dominate, and overcome limitations.
* **The Übermensch:** The goal for humanity; an individual who transcends the "herd mentality" of traditional morality to create their own values.
* **Perspectivism:** The epistemological stance that there is no "God's eye view" of truth. Everything we know is filtered through the specific perspective of the observer.
* **Eternal Recurrence:** A thought experiment asking: If you had to live your life over and over for eternity, would you choose to act in a way that makes you proud of every single moment?
